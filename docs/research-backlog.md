# Research backlog and promotion criteria

All entries are proposed or unvalidated. Each requires an owner, approved experiment budget, protocol/dataset, and recorded results. Documentation does not allocate funding. Experimental outputs have no pilot routing or payment authority.

## DARCI
Preserve inputs, units, provenance, coordinates, assumptions, uncertainty, model/configuration versions, and applicability. Promotion requires independently reviewed methodology, controlled and field comparisons, predeclared acceptance criteria, reproducibility, failure analysis, and explicit authority approval. Missing inputs remain unavailable.

## 55/45 ensemble
Candidate versioned weighting, not established Bayesian consensus. Require common-target outputs, calibration, error-dependency analysis, baseline comparisons, and disagreement handling. No automatic structural verdict in the pilot.

## WASM
Candidate edge implementation. Define sub-50-ms benchmark operation, supported device/software, cold/warm runs, preprocessing, and tail latency. Define what the $0.008 cost target includes. Promotion requires correctness parity, repeatable benchmarks, resource measurements, and demonstrated advantage. Compilation does not prove physical accuracy.

## Merkle manifests
Define encoding, leaf serialization, ordering, parents, empty/odd-leaf behavior, versioning, and trusted-root storage. Promotion requires interoperability vectors and negative tests for alteration, omission, duplication, and reordering. Integrity is not authenticity or legal admissibility. Ledger anchoring remains optional future research.

## Hardware and quality
M4 iPad Pro is intended hardware. Prove native depth access and alignment before requiring it. Sharpness variance 160 remains configurable and unvalidated; define preprocessing/resolution and measure usability false acceptance/rejection. Validate offline recovery on physical supported devices.

## Promotion governance
Review at pilot milestones. Link implementation, tests, validation reports, limits, operational controls, and authorized scope changes. Preserve historical versions and findings. Research must not bypass consent or human review.
